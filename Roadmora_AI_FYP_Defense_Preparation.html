Thirty-second opening answer

Roadmora AI is a career-guidance web platform for students, graduates, and career changers. A structured assessment is encoded into 376 features and passed to our locally deployed PyTorch neural network, trained from random initialization, which predicts a six-dimensional RIASEC profile. We compare and re-rank that profile against 923 real O*NET occupations using interests, skills, experience, and industry evidence, then attach real O*NET roadmap content and BLS wage and growth data. The result is saved securely through Supabase and can be revisited from the user's dashboard; no external LLM or OpenAI API generates the recommendation.
