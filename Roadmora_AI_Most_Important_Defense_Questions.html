Its current best validation mean percentile rank is about 0.202, where lower is better; this is not classification accuracy.

Strong closing statement

Roadmora is not presented as a perfect career authority. It is a reproducible decision-support system that improves on the base paper through a structured RIASEC foundation, 923 specific O*NET occupations, multi-signal ranking, real BLS market data, and actionable roadmaps, while honestly acknowledging synthetic respondents and U.S.-data localization as limitations.
